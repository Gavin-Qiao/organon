# NeurIPS 2026 main-track formatting and anonymity

Official NeurIPS 2026 main-track requirements verified 2026-08-23: submissions use the year-specific LaTeX style and are double-blind; the main paper has at most nine content pages including figures and tables, while references, optional technical appendices, and the mandatory checklist are excluded. Camera-ready papers receive one additional content page. The submitted PDF is capped at 50 MB. The default style is the anonymous review form; preprint is the non-anonymous public option, while final is reserved for accepted camera-ready papers. Acknowledgments are omitted from anonymous submission.
