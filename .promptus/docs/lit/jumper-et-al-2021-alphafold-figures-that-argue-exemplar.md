# Jumper et al. 2021 (AlphaFold) - figures that argue (exemplar)

The protein-structure paper. The exemplar of figures-that-argue: each figure a self-contained argument (claim panel, evidence panels, failure modes), caption titles stating findings as sentences, uncertainty rendered on the page. Grounds the exemplars reference and the coming editio-figures caption grammar (practice: write the caption's claim sentence before making the figure).
