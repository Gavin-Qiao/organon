# TPAMI author policy 2026 - compsoc template current; 12-page limit; single-anonymous default

IEEE Computer Society TPAMI author policy, live-verified 2026-07-04 via the Template Selector walkthrough + computer.org pages: the template is UNCHANGED - [10pt,journal,compsoc]{IEEEtran} (IEEEtran v1.8b, 2015) + IEEEtran.bst, distributed solely through template-selector.ieee.org; submission is double-column via the template. Regular-paper limit 12 formatted double-column pages incl. references and bios (NOT 14 - our venue data was wrong and is corrected); submissions to 18 pages with USD 220/page overlength (MOPC) past 12; short 8, survey 20. Review: single-anonymous by default, double-anonymous on justified request at the EIC's discretion - editio's blind render is opt-in for TPAMI. Feeds editio/templates/venues/tpami/venue.json.
