# Okabe-Ito palette (Wong 2011)

Wong's Nature Methods column "Points of view: Color blindness" popularized the Okabe-Ito palette (Okabe & Ito 2008): eight categorical colours chosen to stay distinguishable under the common colour-vision deficiencies. editio adopts it twice — as the draft-mode claim tints in editio.sty ([[three-renders-one-source]]) and as the default matplotlib colour cycle for figures — paired with viridis/cividis for sequential data (never jet) and redundant encoding (linestyle, marker) so greyscale print survives. Hex reference: https://conceptviz.app/blog/okabe-ito-palette-hex-codes-complete-reference.
