# NMI initial formatting and optional anonymization

Official NMI initial-submission guidance verified 2026-08-11: the journal accepts Word, TeX/LaTeX, or PDF initially and asks TeX authors to provide a compiled PDF; strict house-style formatting is not required at this stage. Double-anonymized peer review is optional and author-selected, with authors responsible for removing identifying material.
