# LongMemEval separates retrieval updates time and abstention

The ICLR 2025 LongMemEval benchmark contains 500 curated questions testing information extraction, multi-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates, and abstention. Commercial assistants and long-context models showed an approximately 30 percent accuracy drop over sustained histories. Its indexing-retrieval-reading decomposition suggests stage-specific Promptus diagnostics instead of one aggregate recall number.
