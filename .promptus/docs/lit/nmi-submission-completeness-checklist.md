# NMI submission completeness checklist

Official NMI submission-completeness checklist verified 2026-08-11: submit a manuscript file, a cover letter, and optional Supplementary Information. The manuscript must contain author information unless double-anonymized review is chosen, a detailed replicable description including Methods and materials, and a reference list; figures and up to ten optional Extended Data items belong in the manuscript where applicable. Cover-letter and Supplementary Information packaging are separate from the Article section order.
