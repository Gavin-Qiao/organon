# Watson & Crick 1953 - brevity as confidence (exemplar)

One page in Nature announcing the double-helix structure. The exemplar of brevity-as-confidence: the finding stated in the opening paragraph, space spent on implications, a single forward-looking sentence instead of a hedged future-work section. Grounds the exemplars reference in editio-structure (practice: if the introduction's first paragraph does not say what you found, delete what precedes it).
