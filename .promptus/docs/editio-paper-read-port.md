# editio paper read-port

editio is promptus's paper read-port: it renders what the store has VALIDATED into a defensible, submittable paper, the way grannie renders the store for a human conversation. The discipline that makes the store trustworthy — calibrate to the evidence, name your sources, keep your dead-ends — is what a paper needs; editio is the render side of that loop. Every claim on the page traces to a store unit at a known status, and the draft render makes that status visible, so the author sees what is solid and what is air before a reviewer does.
