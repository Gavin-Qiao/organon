# Krzywinski Altman 2013 - error bars must state what they are and n

The error-bar rule editio's caption grammar enforces: a legend must state what the bars represent (SD, SEM, or CI) and n - descriptive and inferential bars are not interchangeable, and the meaning cannot be recovered from the picture alone.
