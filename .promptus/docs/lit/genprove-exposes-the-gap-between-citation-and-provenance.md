# GenProve exposes the gap between citation and provenance

The ACL 2026 GenProve paper argues that citations alone do not show how a source supports a generated claim. Its fine-grained provenance task distinguishes quotation, compression, and inference; evaluated models perform substantially worse on inference provenance than surface quotation. For Promptus this motivates an output-time claim trace test, not mandatory fine-grained metadata on every stored unit.
