Organon is developed by dogfooding Promptus and Editio in Mohan's real long-running projects. Problems, friction, and project-specific workarounds observed in Psi, MoT, Probatio, and later projects are the primary inputs to Organon's roadmap.

The working loop is: reproduce the project problem, recover its evidence, identify the general failure beneath the local symptom, implement the smallest reusable improvement in Promptus or Editio, and validate it against both the originating use and Organon's deterministic gates. Do not manufacture features merely because they sound useful; let real use earn the machinery.

This principle governs the whole Organon marketplace, not only Promptus. Related validated method: [[dogfood-and-mine-same-day-fix-loops-and-agent-team-ledger-mining]].
