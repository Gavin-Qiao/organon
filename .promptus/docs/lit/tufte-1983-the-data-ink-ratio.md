# Tufte 1983 - the data-ink ratio

Data-ink: maximize the share of ink carrying non-redundant data, within reason. Behind the mplstyle's dropped spines and the no-chartjunk defaults in editio-figures. Keep the ink that carries orientation (ticks, labels, the comparison axis).
