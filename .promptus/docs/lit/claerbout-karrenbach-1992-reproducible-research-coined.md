# Claerbout & Karrenbach 1992 — reproducible research coined

Claerbout JF, Karrenbach M (1992), 'Electronic documents give reproducible research a new meaning', SEG Technical Program Expanded Abstracts, 601-604. Coins/canonizes 'reproducible research': the paper plus its exact code+data should regenerate every result on demand. The root claim behind editio-numbers: a reported number is only as good as its traceable source. Verified via Crossref + SEG Library 2026-07.
