# Nature final artwork dimensions and typography

Nature final-artwork guidance used by the NMI profile: original-research figures use 88 mm single-column or 180 mm double-column widths; vector artwork is preferred where appropriate; raster colour images should be RGB and at least 300 dpi; labels normally use a consistent sans-serif typeface at 5–7 pt. Editio chooses the upper 7 pt bound as an authoring default.
