# Nature Machine Intelligence Article limits and structure

Official Nature Machine Intelligence Article rules verified 2026-08-11: main text is at most 3,500 words excluding abstract, Methods, references, and figure legends; the abstract is at most 150 unreferenced words; an Article has at most six display items and typically up to 50 references. The Article body is an unheaded Introduction followed by Results, Discussion, and Methods; Results and Methods may use topical subheadings, while Discussion has none.
