# Three renders, one source

One markdown source produces three artifacts through a single \editiomode switch read by editio.sty. Draft is the author's instrument: claim tints (Okabe-Ito, colourblind-safe — [[okabe-ito-palette-wong-2011]]), provenance stamps (updated + grounds from section front-matter), TODO marks on the page. Publish is camera-ready: every annotation macro collapses to identity. Blind is double-blind: publish plus masked self-citations and dropped \blindhide blocks. The venue class (IEEE, arXiv, Nature, Elsevier, ACM as the seed menu) is orthogonal — swap the class, keep the mode. The point is continuity of honesty for [[editio-paper-read-port]]: the same status visibility that keeps the agent calibrated in the store ([[editio-audit-loop]]) reaches the manuscript page in draft, and vanishes without residue in print. CONJECTURED until the three modes compile-test on a real paper (Phase 6).
